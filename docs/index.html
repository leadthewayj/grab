Share a link. Get the file.

Tap Share on a video in YouTube, Chrome or most other apps and pick Grab. Choose what you want and tap Download. The download runs on your phone and the file saves to your Download folder.

Install

Grab isn't in the Play Store, so Android asks you to allow it once.

  • On your phone, tap Download for Android on this page.
  • Open the downloaded file. When Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then go back.
  • Tap Install, then Open. Grab is now in your share menu.

Chrome may warn that the file could be harmful. It says that about every app downloaded from outside the Play Store.

Good to know

Which phones?

Android 11 or newer on a 64-bit phone, which covers almost every phone made since 2019.

Where do files go?

Download/Grab on your phone. Videos usually show up in your gallery app under Download.

A site stopped working

Sites change often. Grab updates its download engine once a day; to update right away, tap Update the downloader at the bottom of the app.

New versions

Download the newest version from this page and install it over the old one. Your saved files stay where they are.
