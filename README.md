# Grab

A small Mac app for saving audio and video from a link. Paste one link or a whole list, pick what you want, and the files land in your Downloads folder.

**[Download the latest Grab](https://github.com/leadthewayj/grab/releases/latest/download/Grab.zip)**

Needs macOS 13 or newer. Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

## Install

1. Unzip the download and drag **Grab** into your Applications folder.
2. Open it. macOS will say it can't verify Grab, because it isn't from the App Store. Click **Done**.
3. Open **System Settings > Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to Grab. You only do this once.
4. Grab asks to fetch its download engine (about 200 MB). Click **Set Up** and give it a minute.

## What it does

- Audio as MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, or the original with no conversion
- Video as MP4, MKV or MOV, from 360p up to 4K
- Transcript: save what's said in a video as a text file, in a choice of languages
- Thumbnail: save just the cover image
- Trim: keep only part of a video, by typing times or by watching a preview and dragging the start and end
- Pick from a playlist, channel or profile: tick the videos you want
- Following: new uploads from a channel or profile download on their own
- Convert files already on your Mac with the same settings
- A queue with pause, resume and drag-to-reorder that survives quitting the app
- Up to 32 downloads at once
- Presets, download history with search, and a menu bar icon for quick downloads
- Settings for file names, save folder, folder sorting, speed limit, subtitles, cover art and more

## Updating

Grab tells you when a newer version is out. It also keeps its download engine current on its own. If a site stops working, choose **Update Download Engine** from the Grab menu.

## Good to know

- Closing the window leaves Grab running in the menu bar. Quit it from the Grab menu, or turn the menu bar icon off in Settings.
- Running many downloads at once can make a site slow you down for a while. If downloads start failing, lower "Downloads at once" in Settings.

## A note on use

Only download things you have the right to save, such as your own uploads or material you have permission to use.
